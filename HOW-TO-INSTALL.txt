ANGLER FISH RESCUE - putting it on a phone or tablet

The game needs to be on a website once, so the phone can save it as an app.
After that it works offline, with no internet.

1. On a computer, go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and make a free account.
2. Drag the whole "angler-fish-rescue-app" folder onto the page.
3. Netlify gives you a web address (something like https://cheerful-fish-123.netlify.app).

iPhone or iPad
4. Open that address in Safari.
5. Tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
6. Open Spencer from the home screen, turn the phone sideways, turn the sound on.

Android phone or tablet
4. Open that address in Chrome.
5. Tap the three dots menu, then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
6. Open Spencer from the home screen.

Open the game once with internet after installing and leave it open for a minute
(it saves the voices), then it plays offline.
Progress is saved on each device separately.
